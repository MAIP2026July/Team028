## Recognising phishing
Warning signs: urgent tone, requests for credentials or bank details, unexpected attachments, sender addresses that look slightly wrong, and links whose real address (hover to see it) does not match the text.

## Reporting a phishing email
1. Do not click links or open attachments.
2. Click **Report Phishing** in the Outlook ribbon (or the "..." menu in Outlook on the web).
3. If you already clicked a link or entered your password, call the Service Desk immediately on the 24/7 line. Your password will be reset and your sessions revoked.

## Lost or stolen laptop or phone
Report it to the Service Desk within 1 hour, any time of day. IT will:
- Remotely lock and wipe the device through Intune
- Disable the device in Entra ID
- Reset your password
All company laptops are encrypted with BitLocker, so data is protected even if the device is lost. You may need to file a police report for insurance; IT will arrange a replacement device.

## BitLocker recovery key
After BIOS or hardware changes, a laptop may ask for its BitLocker recovery key. Call the Service Desk with the Key ID shown on the screen; after identity verification they will read you the key. You can also find it yourself at https://myaccount.microsoft.com > Devices > View BitLocker keys.
