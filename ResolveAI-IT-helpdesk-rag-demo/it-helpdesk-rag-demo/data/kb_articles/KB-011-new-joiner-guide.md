## Before day one
Your manager raises a New Joiner request at least 5 working days before your start date. IT prepares your laptop, account and standard access. Your account is disabled until your start date.

## First login
1. Your manager receives a Temporary Access Pass (TAP) for you, valid for 8 hours on your start date.
2. Power on your laptop, connect to Acme-Corp Wi-Fi or a network cable, and sign in with your email address and the TAP.
3. Set your own password (see KB-002 for the policy).
4. Register MFA (see KB-003).
If you see "The sign-in method you are trying to use isn't allowed", your account may not be active yet - contact the Service Desk.

## Day-one checklist
- Install the Teams and Outlook mobile apps (optional, on your phone)
- Add the FollowMe printer (KB-005)
- Request access to your team's shared folders via the Access Portal
- Complete the mandatory Information Security training within 7 days

## Equipment
Standard kit: laptop, USB-C dock, headset, mouse and keyboard. Monitors are provided at office desks. Additional equipment is requested through the IT Portal with manager approval.
