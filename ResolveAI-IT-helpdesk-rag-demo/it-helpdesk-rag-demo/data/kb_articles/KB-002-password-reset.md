## Password policy
- Minimum 14 characters. Passphrases (e.g. three or four random words) are encouraged.
- Passwords expire every 180 days. You will be reminded 14 days before expiry.
- You cannot reuse your last 10 passwords.

## Self-Service Password Reset (SSPR)
You can reset your own password at any time, from any device, at https://passwordreset.acme.example.
1. Enter your work email address.
2. Verify using the Authenticator app or your registered mobile number.
3. Choose a new password that meets the policy.
If you are not on the office network, connect to the VPN after logging in with the new password so your laptop updates its cached credentials.

## Account lockout
Your account locks for 15 minutes after 10 failed sign-in attempts. Repeated lockouts are usually caused by a device still using an old password, such as:
- Mail app on your phone or tablet
- Mapped network drives with saved credentials
- Saved Wi-Fi profile on a second device
Update or remove the old password on these devices. The Service Desk can see which device caused the lockout if you need help finding it.

## After changing your password
- Lock and unlock your laptop (Windows + L) while on the office network or VPN.
- Update the password in the VPN client and mobile mail apps.
- Teams and Outlook may ask you to sign in again; this is normal.
